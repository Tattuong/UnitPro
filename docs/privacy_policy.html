Privacy Policy – Lumen

Last updated: August 2026

Lumen is a local notes app. Your notes, coins, and shop preferences are stored on your device. We do not upload your notes to our servers.

Data stored on device

  • Notes, spaces, and pins
  • Coins balance and shop purchases (local record)
  • App preferences: theme, desk, and finish

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing – for in-app purchases (handled entirely by Google)
  • Remote config – we fetch https://api2.blwsmartware.net/R241.json to check In-App Purchase availability (no personal data is sent in this request)

Permissions

  • Internet – in-app purchases and remote config check

Children's privacy

Lumen does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require an account.
